AI

Modern AI learns from data, and much of the best open data comes from volunteers. Communities also build and study models in public, so research doesn't depend on a few large labs.

Why volunteers matter here

Languages left behind

Most of the world's languages have little digital text or speech. Native speakers are the only people who can fix that.

Good labels make good models

Volunteer classifications from Galaxy Zoo, iNaturalist and camera-trap projects become training data for science-ready models.

Openness and accountability

Open datasets and models let anyone check for bias, test claims and build tools for their own community.

What you need

  • A browser and a microphone for Common Voice.
  • Any language you speak well, including regional and minority languages.
  • Python and a free notebook service for hands-on experiments.
  • A Discord or Slack account to join open research communities.
