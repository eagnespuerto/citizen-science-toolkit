Getting started

From curious to contributor

  1. Pick a question you care about

    Birds outside your window, a star you can see, a prime you can test. Interest keeps you going.

  2. Join an established project

    Platforms like Zooniverse, iNaturalist and SciStarter handle training, data quality and credit.

  3. Learn the protocol

    Follow the project's instructions exactly. Consistent methods are what make volunteer data usable.

  4. Talk to the community

    Forums and talk pages are where odd findings get noticed. Many discoveries started as a forum post.
